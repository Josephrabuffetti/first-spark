## Planet, Sun, and cloud maps

- Creator: **Solar System Scope / INOVE**
- Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Downloads: `2k_earth_daymap.jpg`, `2k_mars.jpg`, `2k_earth_clouds.jpg`, `2k_sun.jpg` from the source page's `textures/download/` directory.
- In this game: `assets/earth.jpg`, `assets/rocky.jpg`, `assets/clouds.jpg`, `assets/sun.jpg`.
- Changes: resized and compressed for loading; sphere projection, lighting, cloud blending, and a temporary desaturated land treatment before microbial life. Mars illustrates the rocky stage, Earth illustrates the ocean and living stages, and the Sun illustrates cosmic energy. These are artistic abstractions, not literal historical reconstructions.
- Source imagery is based on NASA data, processed and published by Solar System Scope. Credit belongs to the texture creators as above.

Attribution is also included in the in-game guide. No endorsement by either creator is implied. No AI-generated imagery is included in this release.

# Next patch: a world that changes through time

Requested after the v5 art and life-building-block expansion:

- A visible planetary year counter, with a clearly explained relationship between gameplay and elapsed world time.
- Random planetary events as a world progresses, accompanied by readable event descriptions and visible scene changes.
- Species evolution driven by a world's conditions and history.
- Volcanic eruptions with visual effects and environmental consequences.

These are future simulation mechanics, not current v5 features. The existing Volcano discovery is a reusable ingredient; it does not currently trigger random eruptions.

Preserve existing progress, keep reduced motion usable, avoid punishing hidden events, and make event outcomes visible before introducing irreversible consequences.

# v7: a world that changes through time

Implemented:

- Saved planetary years, advancing in explicit 1,000-year steps; optional running time advances every eight visible, unblocked seconds.
- Seeded random events every 3,000 years: eligible eruptions, rain, microbial blooms, meteors, and quiet ages.
- Visible event effects, environmental values, bounded history, and four microbial lineage adaptations gated by discoveries and conditions.
- In-scene time controls, replayable formation, brighter moving gas streams, independently moving cloud wisps, faster orbital fragments, and longer major formation sequences.
- Legacy saves migrate automatically. No offline progression or discovery loss. Menus and hidden tabs pause time; reduced motion retains textual outcomes.

## Future development

Deeper ecological interactions, diverse multicellular organisms, persistent terrain changes, and richer planetary biomes. Current evolution is a compressed fictional model of microbial adaptation, not a prediction of actual evolutionary timing. Real-device performance and touch/accessibility testing remain important follow-up work.
